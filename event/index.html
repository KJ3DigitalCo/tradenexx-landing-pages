Questions

The honest answers.

Is this an investment scheme?

No. You do not give us money to trade. This is education, tools and coaching. Your trading money stays in your own broker account, in your own name.

Where and when is it?

The date and venue are announced soon. People who register hear first.

What should I bring?

Your laptop. A Windows laptop works best. We help you set up MT5 in the room, and the full details come with your confirmation.

Do I need trading experience?

No. We start from the basics and you practice on a demo account first, so you can learn without risking real money.

Will I earn money?

We cannot promise that, and anyone who does is a red flag. Trading can lose money, and past results never guarantee future ones. You learn how to manage risk and judge claims for yourself.

Can I join the online webinar instead?

Yes. There is a separate 4-session online webinar for the same price. You can find it from the link at the bottom of this page.
